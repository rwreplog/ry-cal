# Product Specification

## Vision
Replogle HQ answers: **What's going on with the Replogle household?**

The primary dashboard should make it immediately obvious:
- What's happening today?
- Where does everyone need to be?
- What chores need attention?
- What's coming up?
- What does the family need to remember?

## Modes
### Dashboard Mode
Optimized for TV, wall display, tablet, and desktop. Primarily read-only, large type, high contrast, automatic refresh, configurable widgets.

### Admin Mode
Optimized for phone, tablet, and desktop. Used to edit chores, family members, widgets, themes, announcements, and integrations.

## Initial widgets
1. Clock / Date
2. Google Calendar
3. Chores
4. Weather
5. Announcements

## Future widgets
Meal Plan, Shopping List, Countdown, Birthdays, Family Goals, Photos, Sports, School Schedule, Smart Home, Scripture / Quote, Package Tracking.

## Chores
Support assignment, recurrence, due time, completion, and completion history.

## Themes
Potential themes: Modern, Cozy, Retro, Space, Adventure, Medieval, Seasonal.

## Product personality
Modern, warm, fun, personal, slightly playful, highly usable.
